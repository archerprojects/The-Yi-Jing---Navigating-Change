雜卦 — The Hexagrams in Irregular Order

Original text with the translation of James Legge (1882). One of the Ten Wings (十翼) of the YiJing.

《乾》刚《坤》柔。

Strength in Qián, weakness in Kūn we find.

《比》乐《师》忧。

Bǐ shows us joy, and Shī the anxious mind.

《临》《观》之义，或与或求。

Lín gives, Guān seeks - such are the several themes
Their different figures were to teach designed.

《屯》见而不失其居。

Zhūn manifests itself, yet keeps its place;

《蒙》杂而著。

'Mid darkness still, to light Méng sets its face.

《震》，起也。《艮》，止也。

Zhèn starts; Gèn stops.

《损》《益》，盛衰之始也。

In Sǔn and Yì are seen
How fulness and decay their course begin.

《大畜》，时也。

Dà Xù keeps still, and waits the proper time.

《无妄》，灾也。

Wú Wàng sets forth how evil springs from crime.

《萃》聚而《升》不来也。

Good men in Cuì collect; in Shēng they rise:

《谦》轻而《豫》怠也。

Qiān itself, Yù others doth despise.

《噬嗑》，食也。

Shì Hé takes eating for its theme;

《贲》，无色也。

Bì takes what is plain, from ornament quite free.

《兑》见而《巽》伏也。

Duì shows its scope, but Xùn's we do not see.

《随》，无故也。

Suí quits the old;

《蛊》则饬也。

Gǔ makes a new decree.

《剥》，烂也。

We see in Po its subject worn away;

《复》，反也。

And Fù shows its recovering from decay.

《晋》，昼也。

Above in Jìn the sun shines clear and bright;

《明夷》，诛也。

But in Míng Yí 'tis hidden from the sight.

《井》通而《困》相遇也。

Progress in Jǐng in Kùn encounters blight.

《咸》，速也。

Effect quick answering cause in Xián appears;

《恒》，久也。

While Héng denotes continuance for years.

《涣》，离也。

Huàn scatters;

《节》，止也。

But Jié its code of rules uprears.

《解》，缓也。

Relief and ease with Xiè are sure to come;

《蹇》，难也。

Hard toil and danger have in Jiǎn their home.

《睽》，外也。

Kuí looks on others as beyond its care;

《家人》，内也。

Jiā Rén all includes within its sphere.

《否》《泰》，反其类也。

While Pǐ and Tài their different scopes prefer,

《大壮》则止，《遯》则退也。

Dà Zhuàng stops here as right; withdraws Dùn there.

《大有》，众也。

Dà Yǒu adhering multitudes can show;

《同人》，亲也。

Tóng Rén reflects their warm affection's glow.

《革》，去故也。

The old is left by Gé.

《鼎》，取新也。

Dǐng takes what's new.

《小过》，过也。

Xiǎo Guò exceeds;

《中孚》，信也。

Sincere is Zhōng Fú.

《丰》，多故也。

Fēng tells of trouble;

亲寡《旅》也。

Lǚ can boast few friends.

《离》上而《坎》下也。

Fire mounts in Lí; water in Kǎn descends.

《小畜》，寡也。

Xiǎo Xù with few 'gainst many foes contends.

《履》，不处也。

Movement in Lǚ, unresting, never ends.

《需》，不进也。

Xū shows its subject making no advance:

《讼》，不亲也。

In Sòng we seek in vain a friendly glance;

《大过》，颠也。

And Dà Guò's overthrown with sad mischance.

《姤》，遇也，柔遇刚也。

Gòu shows a meeting, where the many strong
Are met by one that's weak, yet struggles long.

《渐》，女归待男行也。

In Jiàn we see a bride who will delay
To move until the bridegroom takes his way.

《颐》，养正也。

Body and mind are nourished right in Yí;

《既济》，定也。

All things are well established in Jì Jì.

《归妹》，女之终也。

Guī Mèi reveals how ends the virgin life;

《未济》，男之穷也。

Wèi Jì how fails the youth (to get a wife).

《夬》，决也，刚决柔也。

The strong disperse the weak; Guài teaches so.

君子道长，小人道忧也。

Prospers the good man's way; to grief all small men go.
